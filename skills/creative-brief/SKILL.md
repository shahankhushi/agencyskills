# Production-Ready Creative Brief

In performance advertising, creative is the primary targeting lever. Algorithms (Meta Advantage+, TikTok Smart Performance, Google PMax) optimize audience matching based on who watches and engages with the first 3 seconds of the creative.

A vague creative brief produces bland, unconvertible ads. This skill generates bulletproof, production-ready creative briefs that give video editors, motion designers, UGC creators, and copywriters the exact visual cues, audio prompts, and psychological triggers required to produce winning assets on the first cut.

## 2. Core Creative Architecture

Every high-performing performance creative follows the **H-M-P-O Framework**:

```
0s ---------------- 3s ---------------- 15s ---------------- 25s ------------- 30s
|     HOOK (H)      |   MECHANISM (M)    |    PROOF (P)     |     OFFER (O)    |
| Stop the scroll   | Explain the 'Why'  | Social proof,    | Direct CTA, risk |
| Visual pattern-   | Agitate problem &  | clinical data,   | reversal, bundle |
| interrupt + audio | reveal solution    | customer results | or promo push    |
```

### The 5 High-Conversion Angle Categories:
1. **Problem / Agitation / Solution:** Direct attack on an ignored daily pain point.
2. **The "Us vs Them" Teardown:** Direct side-by-side comparison against category incumbents or outdated habits.
3. **The Contrarian / Counter-Intuitive Truth:** Shocks the viewer by disproving a common belief (e.g., *"Why your daily green juice is actually making you bloated"*).
4. **The "Show, Don't Tell" Demonstration:** Unmistakable visual proof (e.g., water test, drop test, speed comparison).
5. **The Raw UGC / First-Person Confession:** Looks and sounds like an authentic personal video, not an ad.

## 3. Step-by-Step Briefing Protocol

### Step 1: Establish the Single-Minded Proposition (SMP)
Identify the ONE core promise this ad must communicate. If you try to say three things, the ad says nothing.

### Step 2: Engineer 3-5 Distinct 3-Second Hooks
Because 70% of viewers drop off before second 3, the brief must specify at least 3 distinct hook variations for the same core body:
- **Hook 1 (Visual Pattern-Interrupt):** Unusual camera angle, unexpected physical action, or high-contrast visual.
- **Hook 2 (Text-Driven Curiosity Gap):** High-impact text overlay that stops silent scrollers.
- **Hook 3 (Direct Callout / Problem Hook):** Immediately calls out the exact ICP and their secret frustration.

### Step 3: Script the Second-by-Second Storyboard
Define visual action, on-screen text (OST), spoken audio (VO), and sound effects (SFX) in a synchronized grid.

### Step 4: Specify Aspect Ratios & Technical Delivery Specs
- **9:16 Vertical Video (1080x1920):** Primary for Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts. Specify UI Safe Zones (avoid bottom 20% and right edge where app buttons overlay).
- **4:5 Feed Video (1080x1350):** Standard Instagram & Facebook Feed.
- **1:1 Square (1080x1080):** Multi-placement fallback.
- **Color Profile & Audio:** Rec.709, AAC stereo 48kHz, voice normalized to -14 LUFS.

## 4. Technical Guardrails & Safe Zones
- **Vertical Safe Zone:** Keep all critical text, logos, and faces within `Y: 250px` from top and `Y: 420px` from bottom to prevent UI clipping by Reels / TikTok engagement buttons.
- **Captions:** Hardcode kinetic burned-in subtitles (Font: Montserrat Bold or Proxima Nova, yellow/white with subtle black drop shadow).
- **Music:** Low-volume background lo-fi or high-energy upbeat synth; VO must be at least +6dB louder than music track.

## 6. Verification Checklist

Before releasing the brief to the production team:
- [ ] Single-Minded Proposition (SMP) is clear and limited to one core idea.
- [ ] At least 3 distinct hook variations provided.
- [ ] On-screen text is under 7 words per frame.
- [ ] Technical safe zones and audio normalization standards (-14 LUFS) specified.
- [ ] Deliverable naming convention is explicitly documented.
