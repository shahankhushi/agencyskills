# New Business Engine: RFP Response & Pitch Architecture

Agencies do not win pitches with generic capabilities decks showcasing team photos and vanity awards. Agencies win pitches by identifying the **incumbent agency's blind spot**—the exact points of failure in their current ad account or funnel—and presenting a concrete, mathematical 90-day turnaround plan.

This skill structures audit-led pitch proposals and RFP responses that convert skeptical prospective clients into long-term retainers.

## 3. RFP Qualification Scorecard (Should We Bid?)

Before spending 30 hours preparing an RFP response, evaluate the opportunity against the **AGENCY BANT Filter**:
- **Budget:** Does the prospect have at least $30k/mo ad spend or $8k/mo retainer budget? (If no -> Reject).
- **Authority:** Are we speaking with the decision-maker (Founder, CMO) or an intern running a vendor price check? (If no decision-maker access -> Reject).
- **Need:** Is their growth blocked by marketing execution rather than fundamentally broken product-market fit?
- **Timeline:** Is the decision deadline within 30-45 days?

## 3. The 90-Day Turnaround Roadmap

### Month 1: Foundation, Tracking & Waste Elimination (Days 1 - 30)
- Complete server-side tracking audit (Meta CAPI, GA4, GTM).
- Eliminate bottom 20% wasted ad spend, immediately recovering $12k-$15k in monthly cash flow.
- Launch `client-context.md` onboarding document, codifying brand guardrails and target unit economics.
- Deliver Sprint 1: 4 net-new video concepts (12 hook variations) attacking customer objections.

### Month 2: The Creative Testing Engine (Days 31 - 60)
- Deploy the isolated Creative Testing Sandbox (DCT framework).
- Isolate top 2 winning hooks and scale spend by 25% while defending CPA.
- Launch Google Performance Max with tailored lifestyle and search asset groups.
- Establish bi-weekly creative iteration cycle.

### Month 3: Scaling & Channel Expansion (Days 61 - 90)
- Graduate proven creative winners into high-capacity Advantage+ / CBO scaling campaigns.
- Expand into TikTok Ads or Whitelisted Creator campaigns.
- Target Milestone: Achieve $[Target Revenue] at Blended MER $\ge$ [Target MER].

## 5. Agency Pitch Best Practices

1. **Never Show Capabilities Before the Audit:** If you spend the first 10 minutes talking about your agency, you lose. Open immediately with their numbers, their ad examples, and the specific leaks you uncovered.
2. **Bring Finished Assets to the Pitch:** Show mockups of 2 net-new video ads you wrote specifically for their brand during the presentation. This instantly proves you outwork and outthink every competing agency.
