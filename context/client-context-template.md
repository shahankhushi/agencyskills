## 3. Ideal Customer Profiles (ICPs)

### Primary ICP: "The Marathon & Ultra Grinder"
- **Demographics:** Age 28-48, 60% Male / 40% Female, HHI $110k+, trains 6+ hours/week.
- **Core Agony:** Mid-run cramping, stomach distress from gut-bomb commercial energy drinks, and dehydration headaches.
- **Failed Alternatives:** Liquid I.V. (too sugary), LMNT (too salty/harsh on stomach), Gatorade (artificial junk).
- **Buying Trigger:** Training for an upcoming race; suffered dehydration collapse or severe cramp in recent long run.
- **Primary Objections:** "Is it actually third-party tested?", "Will this cause gastrointestinal distress?", "$2.20 per stick is expensive."

### Secondary ICP: "The CrossFit & Hyrox Competitor"
- **Demographics:** Age 24-38, high intensity interval athletes, fitness community members.
- **Core Agony:** Power drop-off in round 4/5, slow recovery between double-day sessions.
- **Buying Trigger:** Transitioning to high-volume summer training or prepping for a competition.
